Common Questions

Frequently AskedQuestions

How quickly can you provide a shortlist? +
Our standard SLA is 48 hours from when we receive your full requirement — territory, CTC band, division and joining timeline. For urgent requirements with immediate joining, we can often deliver a shortlist within 24 hours depending on the HQ and division.
What is your replacement guarantee? +
If an MR we place exits within the replacement SLA period (typically 60–90 days, agreed upfront), we source a replacement at no additional recruitment fee. The replacement shortlist is delivered within 5–7 working days of the exit being confirmed.
Do you cover Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets? +
Yes — and this is one of our strongest capabilities. We have active candidate pipelines in Kalahandi, Nuapada, Bargarh, Koraput, Jharsuguda and Balangir. Most national staffing agencies won't deploy here profitably. We specialize in exactly these markets.
What product divisions do you recruit for? +
We recruit for all major divisions: Allopathy/Ethical, Generic, Ayurvedic/Herbal, OTC, Cardiology, Diabetology, Dermatology, Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Orthopaedics, Neurology, Oncology, Gastroenterology and PCD/Franchise. Our candidate database is tagged by division for accurate matching.
How does the payroll outsourcing work? +
You pay us one consolidated invoice monthly — covering MR salary, TA/DA and our management fee. We disburse salary directly to MR bank accounts, generate payslips, and handle all related paperwork. GST applies only on our management fee, not the full billing amount.
Is there an upfront fee to get started? +
No upfront fee. We discuss our pricing structure only after understanding your requirement. For recruitment, our fee is typically charged upon successful joining of the candidate. For payroll outsourcing, billing begins from the first month of deployment.
Can you handle compliance (PF, ESIC, offer letters)? +
Yes. We provide offer letters and appointment documents from day one. PF and ESIC compliance is added as your MR headcount scales — typically from 20+ employees. We work with CA support for statutory filings.
Do you only work in Odisha? +
Our primary focus and deepest knowledge is Odisha. However, we can support deployments in neighbouring states — Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and West Bengal — for clients with multi-state requirements. Contact us and we'll be honest about our coverage in your target area.
