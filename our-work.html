Candidate Quality

What WeScreen For

Every candidate on our shortlist has been personally evaluated across these three dimensions — before they reach you.

🗣️
Communication
Can this MR communicate confidently with GPs and specialists? We assess verbal fluency, confidence and product articulation ability.
🗺️
Territory Knowledge
Does the candidate know the key doctors, chemists and stockists in their target HQ? Local knowledge is non-negotiable.
📊
Stability Track Record
We review tenure patterns carefully — job-hoppers are flagged. You want MRs who will build doctor relationships, not restart every 6 months.
