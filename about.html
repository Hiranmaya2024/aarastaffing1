Who We Are

Pharma Insiders,Not Recruiters.

Aaradhya Sales Force Solutions was built by people who grew up in the pharma distribution ecosystem — not by HR generalists.

Our Story

Built from the Ground Up,Inside Pharma

Aaradhya SFS was founded out of a simple observation: the pharma companies that struggle most with field force aren't struggling because of a talent shortage. They're struggling because the agencies meant to help them don't understand the industry.

Most staffing agencies treat pharma recruitment the same as placing any other sales role. Our background is in pharma wholesale and distribution — which means we understand the stockist networks, doctor clusters, and territory dynamics that shape whether an MR succeeds or fails.

💡 Our Core Belief

A great MR placed in the wrong territory will fail. The agency's job isn't just to find a person — it's to ensure the deployment works.

🌍 Our Geography

We are Odisha-first. Our deep knowledge of districts, HQs, and stockist networks across the state is what makes us genuinely useful.

🤝 Our Commitment

Every client gets a single point of contact who knows their business. Not a CRM ticket. A real person who answers the phone.
